Mission

WHY BUILD WITH 7TRIBES?

MISSION

Use your skills to help develop practical systems that strengthen communities, expand opportunity, connect people, and support local economic activity.

REAL-WORLD WORK

Builders can contribute to technology, business development, education, merchant onboarding, community programs, communications, operations, and future infrastructure projects.

COMMUNITY IMPACT

The objective is not simply to build another website or application. The objective is to build useful infrastructure that communities can actually participate in and benefit from.

Participation models

HOW BUILDER OPPORTUNITIES WORK

7Tribes may use different participation models depending on the project, organizational needs, available funding, and applicable agreements.

VOLUNTEER

Contribute time or professional skills to support community programs, outreach, education, events, research, or organizational development.

PROJECT / CONTRACT

Specific professional work may be performed under an agreed project scope, timeline, deliverables, and compensation arrangement.

PAID STAFF

As organizational funding and operational needs develop, certain positions may become paid employment opportunities.

ADVISORY & LEADERSHIP

Experienced professionals may contribute through advisory roles, committees, governance, strategic planning, or other leadership responsibilities.

GRANT OR PROGRAM-FUNDED

Some opportunities may be funded through grants, sponsorships, partnerships, donations, or specific community programs.

7TRB PARTICIPATION

7TRB may be used within appropriate ecosystem activities when applicable and mutually agreed upon. It is not guaranteed compensation, equity, ownership, profit sharing, or an investment return.

Participation notice

Participation does not automatically create employment, equity, ownership, token allocation, compensation, or a financial interest in 7Tribes. Any paid position, contract, stipend, grant-funded role, or other compensation arrangement must be separately approved and documented.
