01 / The purpose

How 7TRB Works

7TRB is the 7Tribes community utility token on Alkebuleum. Its intended role is to support participation and exchange within a growing network of people, local businesses, and projects.

What can I actually do today?

Explore Loop, visit Connect, read the token details, and use a compatible wallet to review on-chain 7TRB activity. Some wallet and community features are accessible while merchant payments, rewards, and referrals remain in testing or development.

Does circulating 7TRB make it more valuable?

No automatic price increase comes from moving a token. Market value depends on actual willing buyers and sellers, available liquidity, demand, supply, and risk. Sending tokens between wallets does not itself create new money or guarantee a buyer.

How is 7TRB different from ALKE?

7TRB is the 7Tribes token. ALKE is the network's native asset used to pay blockchain transaction fees on Alkebuleum. Having 7TRB does not automatically mean you have ALKE for gas.

02 / Your wallet

How Nuru Works

Nuru is a separate mobile app developed by Alkebuleum Technology. It combines a self-custody wallet, identity tools, and an AI guide that explains wallet actions. Its availability does not mean every 7Tribes integration is active.

  1. Get the official app.Install Nuru from its developer's official app listing, not an unsolicited link.
  2. Create or restore your wallet.Follow the app's identity and security setup. Carefully secure the Security Key, password, passcode, and any recovery information it provides.
  3. Review your wallet.Check the account, network, token, and transaction details. A wallet address is for receiving compatible assets; never send to an unverified destination.
  4. Explore supported Web3 tools.Where the Nuru browser supports a site, open it and approve only connections or transactions you understand. A site loading does not mean all of its features are integrated.
For 7Tribes members: You can browse 7trb.com, Loop, and Connect without connecting a wallet. Nuru-browser access and wallet connections have been tested, while full 7Tribes wallet, merchant, and rewards integration is still being refined. See current status.
What is Nuru's AI Guide?

Nuru's built-in AI Guide explains app features, wallet activity, swap concepts, security, and recovery steps in plain language. It is educational assistance, not a guarantee or personalized financial advice.

Does Nuru hold my money like a bank?

No. The developer describes Nuru as a self-custody wallet, not a bank or broker. Digital tokens are recorded on a blockchain, and your wallet controls the credentials used to access them. Recovery is subject to the wallet's actual security setup.

03 / The basics

Crypto 101

You don't need to become a trader to understand digital assets. Start with what a token is, how wallets work, what fees mean, and what can go wrong.

What is cryptocurrency or a token?

A crypto asset is a digital asset whose ownership or transfers can be recorded on blockchain technology. Some assets are native coins used by a network; others are tokens created on that network. They are not all money, and their usefulness and risks vary.

What is a blockchain?

A blockchain is a shared digital ledger. It can let people independently inspect transactions and addresses, but a transaction record does not prove a business promise, a token's market value, or the identity of a person behind an address.

What is a wallet and a private key?

A wallet is an app or device that manages the credentials needed to access crypto assets. Your public address may be shared to receive assets. A private key, Security Key, or recovery credential must be protected; whoever gets control of it may control your funds. Follow your wallet's specific recovery instructions.

What are network fees (gas)?

A blockchain charges fees to process certain actions. On Alkebuleum the network fee is paid in ALKE. Fees and transaction success can vary; check what the wallet shows before confirming.

How do swaps, liquidity, and prices work?

A swap exchanges one supported asset for another. Liquidity is how much trading capacity is available at a given price. With little liquidity, even a small trade can move the price sharply or fail. A quoted price is not a guaranteed cash-out value. Verify the actual trading pair, fees, and price impact on the exchange.

Can I undo a transaction?

Many confirmed blockchain transfers cannot be reversed. Confirm the receiving address, network, token, amount, and fee. A small test transfer can reduce mistakes, but it does not eliminate risk.

Why should I care about circulation?

Circulation means value being used and exchanged among people and businesses. It can help a community when real goods, services, and opportunities are involved. Moving tokens around without genuine demand or real activity does not guarantee economic growth.

Before any transaction

Protect yourself first

  • Never send anyone your Nuru Security Key, private keys, password, recovery phrase, or verification code.
  • Use official app listings and confirm the 7TRB contract through Transparency and the network explorer.
  • Verify the network, recipient, asset, and fee. Test with a small amount when appropriate.
  • Be skeptical of guaranteed returns, unsolicited token offers, and promises of instant redemption.

Education only; not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. Token prices and liquidity can change, and funds may be lost.
