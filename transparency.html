Treasury & accountability

What is published

The total token supply and contract address are listed above. A public explorer provides an independent way to investigate on-chain activity.

What is still missing

Verified treasury and liquidity wallet addresses, current allocations, distributed amounts, and a dated reconciliation have not yet been published here.

We will not display made-up treasury balances, a fabricated distribution chart, or proposed profit percentages as actual results.

What about spending and profit allocations?

Earlier planning models described possible spending and profit splits. They were proposals, not verified spending, earned revenue, or distributions. Public reporting will only present actual amounts after they can be supported by records.

Wallet and identity

Nuru is available

Nuru is Alkebuleum Technology's separate wallet app for identity and digital assets. It is available on mobile. You do not need Nuru just to read 7Tribes pages.

7Tribes has tested access and connection from Nuru's dApp browser. That does not mean every wallet, token, or merchant feature is fully integrated or released.

Official Nuru site Google Play

Important distinction

Nuru app: available.

7Tribes wallet connection: tested, still being refined.

7TRB usage: subject to supported networks, real liquidity, and each feature's availability.

Never share wallet recovery information with anyone claiming to represent 7Tribes.
