Liquidity & Token Access

Current options for accessing, trading, or transferring 7TRB tokens.

1. Decentralized Exchange

7TRB is available for trading on JollofSwap. Verify the supported trading pair directly on the exchange before transacting.

Open JollofSwap

2. Peer-to-Peer Transfers

Wallet-to-wallet transfers are available on the Alkebuleum network. Users can send 7TRB directly to any compatible wallet address.

3. Treasury Buyback Requests

No active treasury buyback program is currently guaranteed.

4. Staking or Unstaking

7TRB staking is not currently active.

7TRB is intended for ecosystem utility and community participation. Availability, liquidity, and redemption are not guaranteed. Users should verify all contract and exchange information before conducting transactions.
