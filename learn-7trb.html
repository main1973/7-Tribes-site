How 7TRB Is Intended to Work

01 / The token

What 7TRB Is

7TRB is a community utility token created for 7Tribes on Alkebuleum Mainnet. The published supply is 7,000,000,000 7TRB. The idea is to make participation useful as people organize, support businesses, and build projects. Supply and contract information must be verified against the chain.

What does Organize • Build • Circulate mean?

Organize: Meet and work with people through Loop. Build: Discover merchants, services, and projects through Connect. Circulate: Use supported exchanges or future 7TRB community programs when they are actually active. Each step needs real participants and usable products, not just wallet transfers.

Does circulation guarantee the token price will rise?

No. Circulation means movement and potential use, not automatic appreciation. A token's trading price depends on genuine demand, available buyers, liquidity, and market conditions. A fixed supply alone does not establish a market value.

02 / Access

Wallets and Nuru

Nuru is a separate mobile wallet app from Alkebuleum Technology, not a 7Tribes account and not the 7TRB token itself. It can be used to manage supported crypto assets, wallet identity, and transaction information. Its AI Guide explains app actions and security.

How a member gets started
  1. Explore Loop and Connect in a browser. A wallet is not required just to browse.
  2. If you need wallet access, install Nuru on Android or iPhone.
  3. Follow Nuru's wallet creation or restoration steps and secure its Security Key and recovery information offline.
  4. Confirm that the active network supports the token before you receive or send anything. Review the full recipient address, amount, and fee.
  5. Only connect to websites and approve transactions you understand. 7Tribes-specific integrations are still being refined.
What's the difference between ALKE and 7TRB?

ALKE pays blockchain network fees on Alkebuleum. 7TRB is a separate token on that network. You generally need enough of the correct native asset to cover transaction fees when sending tokens.

What is a community treasury wallet?

A treasury wallet would hold project or community assets subject to documented oversight. Multi-approval controls, spending rules, and public reporting are recommended design goals—not claims that 7Tribes has already deployed those controls.

Read the shorter How Nuru Works lesson. Wallet recovery details vary by app; do not assume every wallet uses the same seed-phrase process.

03 / People first

Starting a Tribe

A tribe is a local group working toward a shared goal. Start with an actual community need—not a token price prediction.

  1. Gather people: Start with a manageable group that will show up and take responsibility.
  2. Choose one goal: For example, organize a neighborhood business directory or support a shared local project.
  3. Assign roles: Coordinator, record keeper, and financial steward if money is involved.
  4. Write the rules: Who decides, how disagreements are handled, what happens to funds, and how anyone exits.
  5. Report progress: Keep meeting notes, action items, and receipts so members can check results.

This is an organizing guide, not an active on-chain voting or DAO feature.

04 / Accountability

Treasury: Plans vs. Records

A responsible treasury needs verifiable wallet addresses, opening balances, receipts, spending approvals, and dated reconciliations. A proposal or percentage chart is not proof of funds received or spent.

What should a real treasury report include?

Published wallet addresses, starting and ending balances, transactions, clear categories, authorized decisions, and a way to reconcile on-chain and off-chain records. Any private information should be protected.

How could project funding be approved?

An example process: proposal → review → documented decision → funding if available → milestone delivery → receipt and public progress update. Actual funding depends on separately approved rules and available funds.

Current public reporting gap: verified treasury and liquidity wallet allocation reports are not yet published on the Transparency page. This guide does not claim they exist.

05 / Create value

Builders and Projects

Builders may be developers, organizers, designers, service providers, or other people who contribute real work. Proposed projects should specify the problem, cost, deliverables, and measures of success before any payment arrangement is agreed.

What would a responsible builder project look like?

A written scope, decision-maker, budget, schedule, milestones, review, and proof of delivery. No 7TRB grant or payment should be assumed without an actual agreement and a funded program.

06 / Local commerce

Merchant Participation

Connect can help people discover and connect with businesses. Accepting 7TRB as payment is a separate matter requiring a willing merchant, a working checkout or wallet transfer, clear pricing, refund policies, and a plan for liquidity and accounting.

What is the proposed payment model?

A merchant might choose conventional USD payment with an optional 7TRB component, or pilot a token-based discount or reward. Those are examples of possible arrangements, not available sitewide offers. Merchants decide what they accept and must disclose terms.

What must be confirmed before advertising “7TRB accepted”?

That the specific merchant has agreed, can actually receive the token, has tested checkout and refunds, and has clear exchange-rate, tax, and transaction fee policies. A merchant directory listing alone is not proof of token acceptance.

07 / Real exchange

Trading, Liquidity and Redemption

A blockchain transfer is not the same as a cash-out. To sell or swap tokens, a functioning venue, available trading pair, willing counterparty, and sufficient liquidity must exist.

Can I exchange 7TRB for dollars?

There is no guaranteed 7Tribes USD redemption or treasury buyback program. If an exchange shows a supported 7TRB trading pair, check whether liquidity exists, the fees, the price impact, and what asset you would actually receive. An exchange quote is not a guarantee of conversion to cash.

What about merchant redemption models?

Weekly redemption limits, discounts, and reserve-backed buybacks have been discussed as potential policy ideas. None should be presented as an activated redemption service unless formally funded and launched.

JollofSwap is an Alkebuleum DEX. Verify any available pair and contract before connecting a wallet. Read the disclosure.

08 / Show the work

What to Measure

Useful community reporting would track verified active members, participating merchants, completed work, real transaction activity, and treasury records. Separate on-chain activity from business outcomes.

Where do verified numbers come from?

On-chain transactions can be checked through the Alkebuleum explorer. Member activity, merchant sales, project completion, and cash balances need consent-aware, trustworthy source records. If those records are not available, report not verified rather than making up a number.
